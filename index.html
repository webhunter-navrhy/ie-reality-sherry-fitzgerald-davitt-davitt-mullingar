This impressive four/six-bedroom detached dormer bungalow occupies a private and secluded site of approximately 0.75 acres in the peaceful countryside setting of Gaybrook. Beautifully maintained throughout and surrounded by mature landscaped gardens, the property offers spacious accommodation, a detached garage and excellent outdoor entertaining areas, making it an ideal choice for growing families.

The accommodation opens with an entrance hall finished with solid oak flooring. The main living room is a bright space with a large front-facing window and a solid fuel stove. The impressive kitchen and dining area is a spacious and light-filled room enjoying multiple aspects over the surrounding gardens. The kitchen is fitted with an extensive range of wall and floor units together with a distinctive breakfast bar featuring a brick surround and pine countertop. The dining area provides a door that opens directly onto the decking area.

A separate utility room provides additional fitted storage, while the remainder of the ground floor offers a spacious office could readily serve as an additional bedroom. A second room, currently used as a gym, provides further potential as a bedroom, playroom or additional reception room. A double bedroom and a generously proportioned family bathroom complete the ground-floor.

Upstairs, a spacious landing leads to three excellent double bedrooms, while the principal bedroom is bright and spacious, enjoying triple-aspect windows and its own walk-in wardrobe. A shower room which completes the first-floor accommodation.

Externally, the property is set behind entrance gates on approximately 0.75 acre of beautifully maintained grounds. Complemented by a feature waterfall fountain, a patio and separate decking area to the side. A substantial detached block-built garage.

The property is approximately ten minutes from Mullingar, providing convenient access to the town's extensive selection of schools, and amenities. Mullingar train station provides regular rail connections to Dublin, while the N4 is readily accessible. Local school bus servicing Gainstown and the Downs National school and service for all post primary schools.

Early viewing is highly recommended.
